Dead Sea

Fragments of Salt, a vanishing landscape

The Dead Sea pulls me back again and again. A place where time slows, where the land itself transforms, disappearing beneath the weight of history and crisis. The salt formations rise like quiet sculptures, shifting, dissolving, revealing something new with every visit. I return to search for what remains, for the hidden stories etched in crystal and water. The beauty here is fragile, slipping away, yet in its slow disappearance, it leaves behind moments that feel eternal.
